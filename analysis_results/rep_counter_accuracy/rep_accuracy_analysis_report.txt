IMPORTANT INTERPRETATION
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
These results measure agreement between aggregate robot counts and human video counts.
They do not establish per-repetition classification precision, recall, or F1 because individual robot decisions are not temporally aligned to individual video repetitions in this dataset.

TECHNICAL PERFORMANCE MATRIX ANALYSIS
============================================================================

Participants in dataset: N = 17

ANALYSIS METHOD
----------------------------------------------------------------------------
The four custom technical-performance matrix questions were analyzed descriptively.
For each question, response frequencies (n) and percentages (%) were calculated across the five Likert response categories.
Positive responses = Agree + Strongly agree.
Neutral responses = Neither agree nor disagree.
Negative responses = Disagree + Strongly disagree.
Because these are custom study questions rather than a validated multi-item scale, no combined technical-performance score was created.

QUESTIONS
----------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Exercise Recognition: The robot correctly recognized the exercise I was performing.
2. Corrective Feedback: The robot's corrective feedback matched my actual movement or exercise form.
3. Speech Understanding: The robot correctly understood my spoken responses.
4. Response Speed: The robot responded quickly enough during the exercise session.

OPEN-ENDED RESPONSE ANALYSIS

Technical Error Analysis: Participants reported several perceived technical issues
 during the sessions, most commonly involving repetitions that were not counted during 
 exercises such as push-ups, Russian twists, and T-bar rows. However, review of the recorded 
 sessions indicated that some of these uncounted repetitions were associated with incorrect 
 exercise form or insufficient range of motion, meaning that the absence of a rep count did 
 not necessarily represent a system failure. These cases were therefore distinguished from 
 verified technical failures. Confirmed interaction issues included a speech-recognition failure 
 and a dialogue-state error in which the robot repeatedly requested confirmation after the participant 
 had already responded. Equipment constraints were also identified as a possible contributor to recognition 
 difficulty during the T-bar row.
